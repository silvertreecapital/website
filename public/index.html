Silvertree Capital is a real estate investment holding, pursuing disciplined, long-term investments that perform across market cycles.

About Us

Since 1992, we have invested our own capital in German residential real estate — primarily multifamily — through value-add acquisitions and opportunistic development. No outside investors, no fund structure — decisions are made in-house. We own, manage, and improve our buildings ourselves, and we hold without exit pressure.

Our Approach

We invest in selected opportunities where our market knowledge, hands-on involvement, and long-term perspective let us unlock value. Disciplined underwriting, targeted capex programs, and active leasing drive performance at the asset level — from acquisition through repositioning to long-term ownership.
